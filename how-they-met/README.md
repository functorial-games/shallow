# How They Met

A collection of documented stories about how couples actually met, what made the connection continue, what obstacles mattered, and what turned an encounter into a relationship.

The point is not to extract canned dating advice. Keep the raw human sequence visible:

- where they were and what each person was doing;
- what caused one person to notice the other;
- who initiated contact and how;
- what happened on the first few meetings;
- what they talked or worked on together;
- how they stayed in contact;
- what almost stopped the relationship;
- what changed the relationship's status;
- what they later remembered as important;
- where the record is uncertain or mythologized.

## Source rule

Prefer letters, memoirs, archives, institutional biographies, and serious biographies. Separate documented fact from later interpretation. A famous couple's retrospective story may itself be interesting evidence, but it is not automatically the literal truth of what happened.
