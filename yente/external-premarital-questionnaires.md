# External premarital questionnaires for Yente

**Source class:** external research material, not user-authored questions.  
**Checked:** 2026-11-11.  
**Status:** links and source characteristics indexed; no questionnaire text or live question-bank ingestion implemented.

Yente may refer to these resources when a person explicitly wants premarital discussion prompts. This register is separate from any list of questions the user personally authored. None of the following is a compatibility test, a model of a specific couple, or scientific evidence that a curriculum prevents divorce.

## The original sources

### 1. The Family & Youth Institute — 183 premarital questions

- **Original:** [The FYI's Premarital Questionnaire](https://thefyi.org/the-fyis-premarital-questionnaire/) (published December 13, 2023).
- **Availability:** the complete 183-question list is publicly readable at the publisher's page. This repository links it rather than copying it.
- **Coverage:** personal values and development; expectations of marriage; emotions and intimacy; conflict; family and in-laws; division of labor; money; children; health; religion; intercultural marriage; courtship and wedding customs.
- **Context:** written in an explicitly Muslim marriage-preparation setting; its religious, courtship and gender assumptions should be identified, not silently universalized.
- **Use in Yente:** **core question source**, with provenance and context visible. Discussion topics can be considered when requested, not inferred as personal preferences.

### 2. The Gottman Institute — 52 Questions Before Marriage or Moving In

- **Original:** [52 Questions Before Marriage or Moving In Card Deck](https://www.gottman.com/product/52-questions-marriage-moving-card-deck/). [Gottman Institute article identifying the deck](https://www.gottman.com/blog/newly-engaged-answer-these-questions-before-you-say-i-do/).
- **Availability:** a commercial 52-question card deck. The publisher shows topic descriptions and a few examples, **not an openly licensed complete 52-question text**.
- **Coverage:** romance, social life, work and money, including expectations around living together, division of household responsibilities, and personal preferences.
- **Use in Yente:** **topic map only**. The existing Shallow prohibition on making Gottman-associated material its scientific grounding remains in force. A commercial card deck is not evidence of effectiveness or permission to reproduce its contents.

### 3. FOCCUS — Pre-Marriage Inventory sample questions

- **Original:** [FOCCUS inventory sample questions](https://foccusinventory.com/foccus-inventory-sample-questions.aspx). [Publisher's FAQ](https://foccusinventory.com/foccus-inventory-faq.aspx).
- **Availability:** ten openly viewable sample statements from a larger proprietary inventory. The publisher describes 151 basic and 33 optional items; the full inventory and facilitated reports are **not available here for reuse**.
- **Coverage:** expectations, habits, family problem-solving, faith, children, in-laws, sexual intimacy, financial responsibilities and doubts about the proposed marriage.
- **Context:** a faith-associated, facilitator-administered exercise with versions for different religious contexts. The sample statements use an agree / disagree / uncertain format. Neither access to samples nor an unverified psychometric claim licenses Yente to administer, score or recreate the inventory.
- **Use in Yente:** **sample and comparative topic source**, not a substitute for FOCCUS administration and not a compatibility prediction.

## Source and authorship boundaries

1. Keep **user-authored questions**, **publisher-originated material**, **assistant-adapted questions**, and **new questions explicitly requested by a user** in distinct collections with explicit authorship and provenance. Never silently add generated questions to the user's own list.
2. Link to the originals instead of republishing entire copyrighted inventories. If future reuse is proposed, verify licenses and rights for each item.
3. Obtain a person's permission before presenting prompts. Preserve the freedom to skip or defer sensitive topics; do not conduct an unsolicited interview or use answers as secret matching scores.
4. Do not turn a difference in responses into a diagnosis, an incompatibility verdict, or a prediction of marital survival. Distinguish empirical evidence, religious teaching, commercial claims and personal decisions.
5. Keep any answers, relationship histories or confidential disclosures out of this public repository. Questionnaire indexing does not implement a secure private store or authorize disclosure to another person.
