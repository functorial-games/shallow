# Orthodox and Black church marriage-preparation resources

This is a comparative source map, not a list of endorsed doctrines or empirically proven programs. Religious materials are especially useful when they state plainly what a marriage is supposed to obligate, who has authority to interpret the promise, and what community stands behind it. They are less useful when theological claims are quietly recast as universal psychology or when unsupported statistics are used as sales language.

## Eastern Orthodox and Eastern Christian materials

### *Crowned with Glory and Honor: Guidelines for Orthodox Marriage Preparation*

- Publisher: Department of Marriage and Parish Family Ministries, Antiochian Orthodox Christian Archdiocese of North America.
- Access: free PDF booklet distributed through Ancient Faith.
- Landing page: https://store.ancientfaith.com/crowned-with-glory-and-honor-free-pdf-booklet/.
- Use: official Orthodox preparation framework organized around the mystery of crowning, lifelong fidelity, ecclesial life, sexuality, household formation, and pastoral preparation.
- Shallow value: a clear example of marriage as a sacramental and communal vocation rather than a private compatibility arrangement.
- Caution: preserve its Antiochian Orthodox theological assumptions and jurisdictional context. It is not a neutral counseling manual or outcome study.
- Classification: **comparative commitment source**.

### *The Journey of Marriage in the Orthodox Church*

- Publisher: Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of America.
- Landing page: https://www.goarch.org/-/the-journey-of-marriage-in-the-orthodox-church.
- Companion overview: https://www.goarch.org/-/preparing-for-the-journey-of-marriage.
- Coverage: a couple's book and facilitator material intended for use with a parish priest; theology of marriage, family histories, communication, conflict, sex, children, finances, faith, and life in the church.
- Shallow value: combines explicit sacramental commitments with a structured sequence of practical conversations.
- Caution: the public overview also imports familiar relationship-industry categories such as criticism, contempt, defensiveness, and stonewalling. Screen those claims rather than treating them as the Orthodox program's scientific foundation.
- Classification: **comparative commitment and topic source with citation screening**.

### *With Glory and Honor: Catechesis on the Mystery of Crowning*

- Publisher: Melkite Greek Catholic Eparchy of Newton.
- Link: https://melkite.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/With-Glory-and-Honor-Catechesis-on-the-Mystery-of-Crowning.pdf.
- Coverage: Eastern Christian sacramental theology, communication, sexuality, money, future plans, family, the household as a domestic church, service, and hospitality.
- Useful feature: describes marriage as a form of common ascetic and communal life rather than an arrangement sustained only while it remains emotionally rewarding.
- Caution: Melkite Catholic, not Eastern Orthodox; include it as a neighboring Eastern Christian tradition and label the distinction.
- Classification: **comparative commitment source**.

### Orthodox Family Center premarital program

- Publisher: Orthodox Family Center.
- Link: https://orthodoxfamilycenter.org/pre-marital-program/.
- Structure: six sessions covering canonical requirements, theology of marriage, communication and conflict, family management, finances and stewardship, and children.
- Useful feature: shows the institutional role of priest, parish, and canon law in addition to couple discussion.
- Caution: the page describes a program rather than providing a complete open workbook; use it as a curriculum outline.
- Classification: **topic and institutional map**.

### OCA Diocese of New York and New Jersey, *Concerning Marriage*

- Publisher: Orthodox Church in America, Diocese of New York and New Jersey.
- Link: https://nynjoca.org/files/Guidelines/Marriage_Guidelines_2018.pdf.
- Coverage: canonical and pastoral requirements, preparation, mixed marriages, impediments, second and third marriages, divorce, and ecclesiastical permission.
- Useful feature: makes clear which matters are not left solely to the couple's private preference.
- Caution: primarily a jurisdictional rule source, not a counseling curriculum; dates and diocesan requirements must be checked before practical use.
- Classification: **canonical/institutional source**.

### *Introduction to Coptic Orthodox Marriage*

- Host: Virgin Mary Coptic Orthodox Church, Montreal.
- Link: https://virginmarymtl.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/IntroCopticMarriage-booklet-Third-print.pdf.
- Coverage: partner selection, mixed marriages, mental and physical health, aggression, addictions, finances and debt, children, intimacy, sexuality, and Coptic rules.
- Useful feature: unusually broad lecture notes that put health, aggression, addiction, and debt next to doctrine and family expectations.
- Caution: contains strong gender and doctrinal assumptions and some claims requiring independent verification. It should be studied as a Coptic pastoral text, not adopted as Shallow's clinical or scientific authority.
- Classification: **comparative topic source with strong screening**.

## Black church and African American community materials

The search found fewer complete, openly downloadable Black-church premarital manuals than general evangelical products. That scarcity is itself worth recording. Black-church resources should not be treated merely as racially relabeled versions of generic middle-class counseling: church authority, extended kin, racialized work and wealth conditions, community survival, and the history of state interference with Black family life can alter what preparation needs to cover.

### Alfred Street Baptist Church P3 Premarital Preparatory Program

- Publisher: Alfred Street Baptist Church, Alexandria, Virginia.
- Link: https://www.alfredstreet.org/p3/.
- Structure: ten-week program for engaged couples or couples considering engagement.
- Coverage listed by the church: a Christian model of marriage, communication, conflict, finances, sex, family planning, work-life balance, and wedding preparation.
- Useful feature: locates marriage preparation inside an established historically Black congregation and treats the church as a continuing community around the couple.
- Caution: the page is a program description, not an open curriculum. It uses familiar commercial categories and cites broad claims about divorce and premarital education that must be traced before reuse.
- Classification: **institutional and topic map**.

### FaithTrust Institute, *Black Church Domestic Violence Training Guide*

- Publisher: FaithTrust Institute.
- Link: https://www.faithtrustinstitute.org/resources/articles/DV-Training-Guide-Black-Church.pdf.
- Coverage: domestic violence, theological misuse, pastoral response, safety, referral, and the particular position of Black churches and Black women.
- Shallow value: premarital preparation is defective if it treats abuse as an extreme form of ordinary couple conflict or uses permanence doctrine to pressure someone to remain in danger. This guide supplies a corrective source for clergy and program designers.
- Caution: a domestic-violence training guide, not a complete premarital curriculum. Use it as a required safety layer beside any church preparation program.
- Classification: **safety and institutional-accountability source**.

### Black Church Domestic Violence Institute report, *Mind the Gap*

- Publisher: Black Church Domestic Violence Institute.
- Link: https://bcdvi.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Mind-the-Gap-Final-Report-Black-Church.pdf.
- Coverage: Black clergy, domestic violence, pastoral education, referral capacity, and gaps between congregational influence and professional preparation.
- Shallow value: documents why a church-based commitment framework also needs explicit limits on clergy competence and clear referral pathways.
- Caution: read the full methodology before making prevalence or causal claims. Its direct relevance is institutional preparedness, not proof that any one congregation will respond in a particular way.
- Classification: **institutional safety research**.

### Strong African American Families materials

- Publisher/research base: University of Georgia Center for Family Research and collaborating community organizations.
- Public overview: https://cfr.uga.edu/saaf-programs/.
- Focus: family-strengthening interventions developed with rural African American families, including parenting, racial socialization, couple and family processes, and economic stress.
- Shallow value: not a premarital curriculum, but a useful counterweight to couple-only models that ignore racism, rural institutions, kin networks, parenting, and material conditions.
- Caution: separate the intervention populations and measured outcomes from the needs of engaged couples. Do not claim transfer without direct evidence.
- Classification: **contextual research lane, not premarital counseling**.

## Questions these sources add to Shallow

- What institution or community, if any, promises to support the couple after the ceremony?
- Does the marriage promise concern only the two spouses, or also worship, hospitality, kin, children, and service?
- Who interprets a vow when spouses disagree: the spouses, clergy, canon law, congregation, kin, or civil law?
- Can a religious authority distinguish sacrifice freely undertaken from coercion imposed on the weaker spouse?
- What happens when permanence language conflicts with safety?
- Does the preparation address racialized wealth, work schedules, housing, policing, health, and kin obligations, or does it translate every structural pressure into “poor communication”?
- Are clergy trained to recognize abuse and refer beyond their competence?

## Remaining gaps

- A complete open premarital workbook written specifically by and for Black couples or historically Black congregations.
- African Methodist Episcopal, AME Zion, Church of God in Christ, National Baptist, and Progressive National Baptist denominational manuals that can be legally read in full.
- Ethiopian and Eritrean Orthodox preparation materials in accessible English translations.
- Comparative material from Middle Eastern and African Orthodox communities outside North American diaspora settings.
