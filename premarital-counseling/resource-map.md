# Free and openly readable premarital resources

This is a research map, not a list of endorsed programs. Materials are useful for different purposes:

- **Core question source** — worth mining for concrete questions or exercises after checking assumptions and citations.
- **Legal/economic source** — useful for identifying institutional consequences; law must be checked for the user's current jurisdiction and date.
- **Comparative commitment source** — a religious or cultural account that states what marriage is supposed to obligate; useful for comparison, not neutral advice.
- **Topic map only** — broad coverage but too weak, outdated, commercial, or Gottman-dependent to use as scientific grounding.

## Secular and university-extension material

### ELEVATE: Taking Your Relationship to the Next Level

- Publisher: University of Georgia Cooperative Extension and Auburn University collaborators.
- Access: free couple workbook PDF; reproduction permitted with attribution under the workbook's stated terms.
- Link: https://www.fcs.uga.edu/docs/ELEVATE_CouplesWorkbook.pdf.
- Coverage: knowing one's partner, expectations, commitment, friendship, conflict, goals, money, family, community, affection, and self-care.
- Useful feature: the “10 Ps” inventory asks about personality, passions, plans, previous partners, perspective-taking, problem-solving, family past, health, parenting, and provider potential.
- Caution: screen every attribution and borrowed term. The workbook uses some relationship-industry language also associated with Gottman. Treat the exercises as prompts, not validated causal mechanisms.
- Classification: **core question source with citation screening**.

### National Extension Relationship and Marriage Education Model

- Publisher: Cooperative Extension relationship-education network.
- Link: https://ftp.fcs.uga.edu/docs/NERMEM_HR.pdf.
- Coverage: care for self, choose, know, care, share, manage, connect; cultural and economic context; program design.
- Useful feature: explicitly recognizes that couple-centered assumptions do not fit every culture and that low-resource couples face structural barriers not solved by communication lessons.
- Caution: a conceptual framework, not proof that programs built from it improve long-term outcomes.
- Classification: **research framework**.

### Marriage Survival Curriculum

- Publisher: Utah State University Extension.
- Link: https://extension.usu.edu/hru/files/Brower-et-al-2017-Marriage-Survival-Curriculum.pdf.
- Coverage: commitment, communication, strengthening the relationship, financial compatibility, blended families, and safe relationships.
- Useful feature: broad lesson architecture and instructor activities.
- Evidence caution: the reported pilot relies heavily on retrospective pre/post self-report and a modest convenience sample. It can show that participants said they learned something; it cannot establish durable marital effects.
- Classification: **topic map and activity source**.

### Utah Marriage Handbook

- Publisher: Utah State University Extension.
- Landing page: https://extension.usu.edu/strongermarriage/utah-marriage-handbook.
- PDF: https://extension.usu.edu/files-ou/utah-marriage-handbook.pdf.
- Coverage: expectations, communication, sexuality, money, work and family, housework, parenting, in-laws, remarriage and stepfamilies, domestic violence, addiction, and mental health.
- Useful feature: unusually broad scope and explicit attention to danger and chronic problems.
- Caution: verify cited studies; do not import Gottman claims or typologies as Shallow's scientific model.
- Classification: **core topic inventory with source screening**.

### Philippine Pre-Marriage Orientation and Counseling manual

- Publisher: Philippine Commission on Population and Development / government premarital program.
- Link: https://calabarzon.cpd.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/PMOC-Manual-Module-1_Copy-For-Printing.pdf.
- Coverage: marriage and family relations, roles, relevant law, sexuality, responsible parenthood, family planning, pregnancy, child care, and sexually transmitted infections.
- Useful feature: shows a public-health and family-policy model rather than a narrowly therapeutic one.
- Caution: jurisdictional, institutional, and cultural assumptions require explicit comparison rather than silent adoption.
- Classification: **comparative public program and topic map**.

## Legal, financial, and institutional preparation

### Texas State Law Library: Premarital Education

- Publisher: Texas State Law Library.
- Link: https://guides.sll.texas.gov/marriage-in-texas/premarital-education.
- Coverage: Texas's premarital-education provisions, waiting-period and fee consequences, and links to state materials.
- Useful feature: begins from actual law rather than a communication style.
- Caution: Texas only; statutes and benefits can change.
- Classification: **legal source**.

### Texas Marriage booklet

- Publisher: Texas Attorney General / county distribution copy.
- Link: https://www.dallascounty.org/Assets/uploads/docs/county-clerk/vital-records/Marriagebooklet.pdf.
- Coverage: marriage and law, children and support, community property, debts, premarital and marital property, wills, and beneficiaries.
- Useful feature: a compact checklist of legal consequences couples commonly fail to discuss.
- Caution: confirm the current official edition and current Texas law before relying on any rule.
- Classification: **legal/economic topic source**.

### Florida Family Law Handbook

- Publisher: The Florida Bar; distributed by Florida clerks.
- Link: https://stlucieclerk.gov/deptforms/marriage/Family-Law-Handbook-English.pdf.
- Coverage: marriage as a legal relationship, property, debts, support, children, domestic violence, dissolution, and prenuptial agreements.
- Useful feature: makes the possibility and consequences of dissolution part of preparation rather than treating divorce as an unmentionable failure.
- Caution: Florida only; check date and current statutes.
- Classification: **legal/economic source**.

### Washington Family Law Handbook, marriage edition

- Publisher: Washington State Courts.
- Link: https://www.courts.wa.gov/newsinfo/content/pdf/flhbmarriageedition.pdf.
- Coverage: civil marriage, property and debt, insurance, taxes, inheritance, prenuptial agreements, parentage, and dissolution.
- Useful feature: clear institutional overview suitable for extracting a general legal checklist.
- Caution: Washington only; verify current law.
- Classification: **legal/economic source**.

### Look Before You Leap

- Publisher: University of Wisconsin Extension.
- Link: https://dodge.extension.wisc.edu/files/2010/06/Look-Before-You-Leap.pdf.
- Coverage: marital property, credit, debt, employment benefits, pensions, insurance, wills, trusts, records, and advance directives.
- Useful feature: treats marriage preparation as household risk planning.
- Caution: old legal material. Use only to identify questions; never present its legal statements as current.
- Classification: **historical topic map only**.

## Jewish materials

### Premarital Counseling for Older Adults

- Author: Rabbi Richard F. Address and/or associated Jewish Sacred Aging materials; guide by Rabbi Stephen Viniar as identified in the document set.
- Host: Jewish Sacred Aging.
- Full guide: https://jewishsacredaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Viniar-Premarital-Counseling-for-Older-Adults.pdf.
- One-page checklist: https://jewishsacredaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Viniar-One-Page-Checklist.pdf.
- Coverage: adult children and blended families, assets and liabilities, prenuptial agreements, care obligations, aging, health, intimacy, religion, residence, death, and divorce.
- Particularly useful feature: asks couples to create an explicit marital commitment statement and distinguishes marriages organized primarily around passionate love from those organized around companionship, friendship, care, and later-life realities.
- Caution: written for older adults and Jewish clergy settings, though many questions generalize well.
- Classification: **core question source and explicit commitment model**.

### 18Doors Jewish Interfaith Wedding Guide

- Publisher: 18Doors.
- Link: https://18doors.org/wp-content/uploads/jewish_interfaith_wedding_guide_2017-UPDATED-2019.pdf.
- Coverage: premarital counseling, ritual choices, children, religion, families, money, sex, household habits, wills, advance directives, and the historical and contemporary ketubah.
- Useful feature: takes mixed religious identities and negotiated ritual seriously rather than assuming one shared inherited system.
- Caution: a wedding guide, not an evaluated counseling intervention.
- Classification: **comparative and practical question source**.

## Muslim materials

### Premarital Counseling and Nikah Contract Writing Guide

- Publisher: Boston University open repository.
- Link: https://open.bu.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/f50db1b4-418c-4a53-8d4d-b9685a8d2ade/content.
- Coverage: Islamic marriage contracts, consent, mahr, property, paid and unpaid labor, residence, extended family, household work, monogamy or polygyny, divorce authority, care work, conflict, stipulations, and later renegotiation.
- Particularly useful feature: refuses to pretend that a generic contract is neutral. It directly discusses the gender hierarchy of premodern jurisprudence and shows how couples can make obligations explicit in a contemporary contract.
- Important principle: inability to discuss a proposed term honestly can itself be a reason to postpone or reconsider the marriage.
- Classification: **core contract-and-commitment source**.

### Islamic Foundation Premarital Questionnaire

- Publisher: Islamic Foundation, Villa Park, Illinois; version marked updated August 2024.
- Link: https://www.islamicfoundation.org/pdf/if-premarital-questionnaire.pdf.
- Coverage: 106 questions on expectations, religious practice, roles, prior relationships, health, abuse, children, debt, credit, income, in-laws, friends, affection, apology, anger, mediation, polygyny, and divorce.
- Useful feature: unusually concrete question inventory, including matters many secular workbooks avoid.
- Caution: religious and gender assumptions need to remain visible; do not convert answers into a compatibility score.
- Classification: **core question source with explicit doctrinal context**.

### Ahmadiyya Muslim Community USA premarital handbook

- Publisher: Rishta Nata Department, Ahmadiyya Muslim Community USA.
- Link: https://rishtanata.us/upload/resources/PMC_for_Candidates-Families_April_2015.pdf.
- Coverage: consent and forced-marriage screening, mahr, family participation, housing, financial expectations, and marital roles.
- Useful feature: shows how counseling operates when marriage is embedded in a matchmaking and family institution.
- Caution: one specific Muslim movement, not a general statement of “Islamic marriage”; some legal and theological claims are contested by other Muslims.
- Classification: **comparative institutional source**.

## Christian materials

### Catholic PREPARE/ENRICH couple workbook

- Host: Archdiocese of Baltimore.
- Link: https://www.archbalt.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/catholic_couples_workbook.pdf.
- Coverage: expectations, conflict, finances, sex and affection, roles, children, spiritual beliefs, and stepfamilies.
- Useful feature: makes covenant, sacrament, permanence, and church community explicit rather than presenting them as universal psychological facts.
- Caution: based on PREPARE/ENRICH and situated within Catholic doctrine. Use comparatively and inspect every empirical claim.
- Classification: **comparative commitment source**.

### Marriage Preparation Questions and Conversations

- Publisher: Diocese of Grand Island.
- Link: https://gidiocese.org/marriage-preparation-questions-conversations.
- Coverage: meaning of marriage, whether divorce is an option, visions of life in five, ten, and twenty years, faith, sex, children, careers, money, family, trauma, and conflict.
- Useful feature: direct questions about permanence and the couple's actual theology of marriage.
- Caution: Catholic pastoral source, not neutral or experimentally validated.
- Classification: **comparative question source**.

## Useful for topic coverage but not Shallow's evidence base

### Happily Ever After secular premarital workbook

- Link: https://caringcouples.com/starting-pdfs/HEA-Premarital-Secular.pdf.
- Strength: extensive coverage of family history, personality, goals, roles, money, sex, children, religion, friends, recreation, and conflict.
- Exclusion from core grounding: explicitly imports Gottman categories and findings along with RELATE and other marriage-education frameworks. It may reveal common topics in the counseling industry, but Shallow should not inherit its scientific claims or typologies.
- Classification: **topic map only**.

### Before You Tie the Knot

- Publisher: University of Florida IFAS / SMART Couples.
- Strength: organized workshop sequence covering needs, parenting, communication, conflict, finances, and health.
- Exclusion from core grounding: program materials and evaluations draw significantly on Gottman-derived “skills” and short-term self-report outcomes.
- Classification: **topic map and research object only**.

## Material to reject rather than normalize

Some premarital manuals treat wife obedience, sexual access, gender hierarchy, or corporal punishment as duties. Others treat staying together as a successful outcome regardless of coercion or danger. Preserve such documents only when studying the history or ideology of marriage preparation. Do not present coercive rules as culturally diverse “tips,” and do not place them beside ordinary disagreements as if they were equivalent options.

## Missing work

The next sweep should add:

- Orthodox Christian marriage preparation;
- Black church and historically Black family-service materials;
- Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, and secular-humanist preparation;
- disability-centered and neurodivergent couples' preparation;
- LGBTQ premarital legal and family planning;
- remarriage after divorce and widowhood;
- migration, sponsorship, and transnational marriage;
- union, shift-work, military, maritime, and other work patterns that structure absence and household labor;
- curricula written by and for low-income couples without converting poverty into a communication deficit.
